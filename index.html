Now taking our first families, anywhere in Guwahati

A phone call can't tell you if Maa is fine.

You work in Bengaluru, Delhi or Pune, or abroad. Your parents are at home in Guwahati. Every call ends with "we're fine", and you're never fully sure.

Sarovan gives you someone you trust on the ground, who knows your parents by name, checks on them in person, and tells you exactly what's happening.

Every day, every week

Your parents hear from us every day. Once a week it's a home visit; on the other six days, a phone call. The same person every time.

  1. Every day

    Check-in call

    A reminder for their medicines, and a few minutes on how they're feeling and what they need.

  2. Every week

    Home visit

    Your care manager comes home, checks their health and sits down for a proper conversation.

    • Blood pressure
    • Oxygen
    • Sugar
  3. After every visit

    Update on your phone

    Readings and notes from the visit, so you see trends and not just one number. Your parent's own doctor sets the safe range for each reading, and the app flags any reading outside it in amber or red.

  4. Every month

    Medicines and appointments

    We refill what's running low, confirm their medicines and plan upcoming appointments.

Twice a year we arrange a full set of lab tests, collected at home. You pay only the lab's charges.

Between visits, we handle the rest

The small jobs that pile up when you're far away. We take them off your list.

  • Medicines tracked and refilled

    We keep count, reorder before they run out, and make sure the right tablets reach home.

  • Doctor appointments booked

    We fix the slot, remind your parents, and share the outcome with you.

  • Help if they need hospital care

    If a hospital stay is needed, we handle the admission process and insurance paperwork.

  • Bills, bank work and pension paperwork

    The annual pension Life Certificate, bank KYC, utility bills and the forms that always come at the wrong time. We sit with your parents and get them done. We never ask for OTPs, PINs or passwords, and we never handle cash. Your parents always do the last step themselves.

  • Kept safe from phone scams

    We teach your parents what the common calls sound like, and they can ring us before agreeing to anything or sending money.

  • A home safety check

    We walk through the house looking for what causes falls: bathroom floors, loose wires, poor lighting, missing handrails. Then we tell you what to fix first.

  • An emergency file, ready in advance

    Medicines, blood group, conditions, insurance and hospital details on one page, kept current and printed for the house. So nobody is searching drawers at 2 am.

  • Updates and records on your phone

    Every visit, reading, refill and report is saved in the Sarovan family app. You can show the full picture to any doctor, on your phone. See a sample monthly report.

Questions families ask

Is this a nursing or home-help service?

No. We don't provide nurses, maids or full-time attendants. Sarovan is care management. We keep track of your parents' health, handle the admin, and keep you informed.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Guwahati. Wherever your parents live in the city, we'll come to them.

How much does it cost?

We have one simple monthly plan that covers all regular visits, calls and support. We'll share the price on your free call, once we understand your parents' needs.

What costs are not included?

Medicines, lab tests, ambulances, hospital bills and full-time attendants are paid separately. We arrange these for you, but the cost is yours.

How much hospital help is included?

Admission, paperwork and preparing the insurance claim are included for up to 24 hours per hospital stay, twice a year. Beyond that we charge by the hour at a rate we agree with you first, and we always tell you before we start.

What if my parent falls ill or has an emergency?

Call or WhatsApp us. We'll speak to your parent, contact their doctor, and handle hospital admission and insurance paperwork if it's needed. We don't run an ambulance or a 24/7 emergency line yet. In an emergency, call 108 first, then call us. Here is exactly what we do, step by step.

Will you add emergency support later?

Yes. We will start a 24/7 emergency service soon. Until then, in an emergency, call 108 first, then call us. For everyday care, we work with the doctors your parents already see.

Can you care for a parent who is bedridden or has memory problems?

Yes, we can manage their care: health checks, medicines, doctor visits and updates to you. But we don't provide nurses or full-time attendants. If your parent needs daily hands-on care, we'll tell you honestly on the first call.

Only one of my parents lives in Guwahati. Can you help?

Yes. We care for a single parent or a couple.

My parents don't like the idea of outside help. What then?

That's common. Our first visit is just a friendly conversation. We get to know your parents first, and regular care starts only when they're comfortable with us.

How soon can care start?

As soon as your parents are ready. After our call, we visit them at home to get to know them. Regular visits and calls start once they are comfortable with us.

Who visits if my parent's care manager is ill or away?

The other one of us. Whoever is not your parent's care manager comes to the first visit too, so your parent knows us both. If one of us is ill or away, the other visits.

Do you work with my parents' current doctor?

Yes. We book appointments with the doctors your parents already trust, and we share the doctor's advice with you after every visit. We don't offer our own doctor consultations yet.

Will you go with my parents to doctor visits?

Yes, if you tell us 2 to 3 days before the appointment. Either way, we book it, remind your parents and share the doctor's advice with you.

Which languages do you speak?

Assamese, Hindi and English.

How will I get updates?

Through the Sarovan family app. Visit notes, health readings, refills and appointments appear there as they happen. You can add other family members too, so everyone sees the same updates. You can also call your care manager directly.
