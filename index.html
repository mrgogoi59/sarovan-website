Questions families ask

Is this a nursing or home-help service?

No. We don't provide nurses, maids or full-time attendants. Sarovan is care management. We keep track of your parents' health, handle the admin, and keep you informed.

Do you provide emergency or ambulance services?

Not yet. We don't run a 24/7 emergency line today. If your parent needs to be admitted to hospital, we help with the admission process and insurance paperwork. Round-the-clock emergency support is planned for a later stage.

Do you offer doctor consultations?

Not in the current plan. We book appointments with your parents' doctors and share what the doctor said. Teleconsults are planned for later.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Guwahati. Wherever your parents live in the city, we'll come to them.

How will I get updates?

Through the Sarovan family app. Visit notes, health readings, refills and appointments appear there as they happen. You can also call your care manager directly.
